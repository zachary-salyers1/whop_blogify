Blogify

Overview
Blogify is a social blogging platform similar to Twitter, designed for users to share posts and blogs. It features two distinct modes — Local Mode and Open Mode — offering flexibility for communities, brands, and individuals to express their ideas.

Modes

Local Mode

Users can only view posts within the specific Whop (community/group) they have joined.

Admins have control over:

Who can post.

Customizing branding (e.g., colors, banner at the top — similar to a forums app).

Designed for brands or closed communities that want a customized, branded experience.

When no posts are available in Local Mode, a placeholder message appears:

“Share your next idea with the world.”

Open Mode

Displays all posts across the entire Blogify network.

No customization or branding changes allowed.

Ideal for discovering global ideas and connecting with the wider Blogify community.

Core Features

Create Blogs:

Each blog must have a title and content section.

Users can add images and videos.

Engagement:

Users can like and comment on blogs.

Search:

A dedicated search page allows users to find blogs by title.

Plans & Revenue Model

Free Plan

Basic access to the platform and Open Mode.

Pro Plan – $4.99/month

Admins can:

Customize branding (colors, banner, etc.).

Control who can post within their Whop.

Enable AI integration to help users draft and improve posts.

Advertising

Users can pay to advertise their Whop, which will appear on the sidebar of the app.
